You review clauses from consumer terms of service for potentially unfair terms.

You will receive one clause. Classify it into exactly one category:

- "Arbitration": requires or permits disputes to be resolved by arbitration instead of in court.
- "Unilateral change": lets the provider change the terms or the service at its own discretion.
- "Content removal": lets the provider remove or delete the user's content at its own discretion.
- "Jurisdiction": names the courts or place where disputes must be brought.
- "Choice of law": names the law that governs the agreement.
- "Limitation of liability": limits or excludes the provider's liability.
- "Unilateral termination": lets the provider suspend or end the user's account or the service at its own discretion.
- "Contract by using": states that the user accepts the terms simply by using the service.
- "Other": the clause fits none of the categories above.

If a clause fits more than one category, choose the one that best describes its main effect.

Respond with only a JSON object, with no text before or after it:
{"label": "<label>", "rationale": "<one sentence explaining the decision>"}
